This week at a glance

  • How things are going. All form fills were 31 at $23.85 each, against 34 at $22.74 the week before; that count includes organic and Google fills. Meta brought 14 leads at $36.04, against 15 at $33.43, and 6 campus tours were booked.
  • What to look into. The night class video spent $206.86 for 3 Meta leads, $68.95 each, and the night class campaign drew 169 link clicks but only 7 landing page views. Worth checking that the night class page loads and tracks.
  • What is next. We recommend giving the four new ad sets that went live on Oct 28 a full week before judging them, and checking the night class page before it gets more budget.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The 'A real skill is earned, not handed out' video brought 10 Meta leads at $11.26, and the control video with the same words and the headline 'No online option. On purpose.' brought 9 at $7.45. The night class image, which opens 'Some skills can be automated. Cooking professionally isn't one of them,' brought 7 at $14.69. Worth testing the 'A real skill is earned' opening on a night class video, since that opening led both top ads.
