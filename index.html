This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 45 leads came into the CRM this week at $18.54 per lead, up from 34 leads at $24.87 the week before, on about the same total spend of $834.40. The night class campaign is driving the gain.
  • What to look into. Google spent $313.66 this week with no conversions counted, and the search ads' landing page links changed mid-week. We recommend confirming the new pages still report form fills to Google. The appointments tile is zero because no booking calendar is connected yet.
  • What is next. We recommend shifting more of the Meta budget toward the night class campaign, whose two ads brought in 21 and 18 leads, and holding the general campaign at a lower level while its newer ads are judged.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The night class pair brought 21 and 18 leads. Video and image both open 'Some skills can be automated. Cooking professionally isn't one of them,' then promise nine months hands-on at night without leaving your day job. The 'no online option' video, opening 'A screen can show you a knife cut. It can't feel your grip slipping,' brought 6. We recommend testing a current night student on camera on why they kept their job, since keeping your income is the line that works.
