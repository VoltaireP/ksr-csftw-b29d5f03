This week at a glance

  • How things are going. All form fills were 24 at $31.37 each, against 31 at $23.82 the week before; that count includes organic and Google fills. Meta brought 13 leads at $41.91, against 14 at $36.04, and 6 campus tours were booked.
  • What to look into. Meta counted 37 form leads on its side, but only 13 reached the CRM with a Facebook link. Google spent $208.17 with no recorded conversions. Worth checking both tracking paths.
  • What is next. We recommend matching this week's Meta form leads against the CRM before any further budget raise, so the cost per lead reflects every real lead.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The night class video, which opens 'Some skills can be automated. Cooking professionally isn't one of them,' brought 14 Meta form leads at $13.05. The square night class image, which states the hours plainly (Monday to Friday, 4 to 9 pm, next cohort February 8), brought 7 at $10.28, the cheapest. The 'A real skill is earned, not handed out' video brought 6 at $12.96. Worth testing a night class video that opens with those plain facts, since the plain image is the cheapest.
