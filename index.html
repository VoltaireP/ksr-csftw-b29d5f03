This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Leads in the CRM were 30 at $26.17 each, down from 42 at $20.25 the week before. Ad spend was $784.98, down from $850.59.
  • What to look into. Google spent $315.78 and recorded 2 conversions, both from the keyword search campaign. Branded search spent $103.54 and the automated Google campaign $37.35 with no conversions recorded.
  • What is next. We recommend testing a lower branded search budget and moving that money to the night class campaign on Meta, which brought 23 leads counted by Meta at $12.26 each.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The night class video brought 19 leads counted by Meta at $12.11. It opens 'Some skills can be automated. Cooking professionally isn't one of them,' then offers nine months of chef-led night training while you keep your day job. The 'A real skill is earned' video brought 5 at $18.64, and the night class image with the same words as the top video brought 4 at $12.20. Worth testing a night class video that opens with 'keep your day job', since that offer is what the top ads share.
